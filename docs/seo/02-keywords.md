# Keyword Research & Keyword-to-URL Map

**Read this first: no search volumes or difficulty scores are given.** No keyword tool (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush) was available during this work, and the brief rules out inventing numbers. "Competition" below is a **qualitative judgement** of who ranks today (big OTAs vs. local operators), not a measured score. Get real numbers in 10 minutes once launched:

1. **Google Keyword Planner** (free with a Google Ads account, no spend needed): paste each keyword group, location = India / Maharashtra, language = English **and** Marathi.
2. **Search Console → Performance**, 4–8 weeks after launch: the real queries you appear for. These beat any tool's estimates.

## How keywords were prioritised

GuruPriya's real, sellable product is **group pilgrimage yatras departing Latur** (Char Dham, Kainchi Dham, with Tirupati and South India planned), sold by phone/WhatsApp, with no online prices. So priority = **business value × realistic chance to rank**, not raw volume:

- **P1 Win now**: local + pilgrimage + "from Latur/Marathwada". Low competition, high intent, matches the product exactly.
- **P2 Build towards**: pilgrimage guides that national OTAs rank for, where a specialist with first-hand experience can compete over time via helpful content.
- **P3 Not now**: generic holiday/international terms. Dominated by MakeMyTrip/Thomas Cook/Yatra, and GuruPriya doesn't sell them.

Intent: **T** transactional (wants to book/enquire) · **C** commercial (comparing operators/packages) · **I** informational · **N** navigational (brand).
